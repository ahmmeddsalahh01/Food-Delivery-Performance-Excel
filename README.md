# Food Delivery Business Performance Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard built with Power Pivot, DAX, and PivotTables that analyzes a food delivery business across 8 Egyptian cities: sales, order funnel, delivery speed, customers, restaurants, and riders.

## Business question
How is the delivery business performing, where do sales come from, and which restaurants and riders perform best?

## Tools
Excel, Power Pivot, DAX, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers

## Data model
Five tables in Power Pivot: orders, restaurants, riders, customers, and a calendar table.

## Data
Dataset: generated for practice. Five tables in Power Pivot: orders, restaurants, riders, customers, and a calendar table.

## Key measures (DAX)
Total Sales of Delivered Orders, Total Orders, Cancellation Rate, Refund Rate, Average Order Value, Average Delivery Time, % of Orders Delivered Within a Target Time, Average Rider Rating, and Orders Per Customer.

## Dashboard
Five sheets (Revenue & Growth, Order Funnel, Delivery Performance, Customer Insights, Restaurant & Rider Performance) with 30+ PivotTables, 12 charts, and slicers.

## Key findings
Delivered sales totaled about EGP 2.13M, with an average order value of about EGP 355.
- **Italian (20.6%) and Asian (19.1%)** cuisines generate about 40% of delivered sales.
- **Aswan** is the top city (about EGP 431K, roughly 20% of sales), while **Cairo** is the lowest (about EGP 142K, roughly 7%).

## Recommendations
1. Focus promotions on Italian and Asian cuisines.
2. Investigate why Cairo trails every other city.
3. Work with restaurants that have the highest cancellation rates.
